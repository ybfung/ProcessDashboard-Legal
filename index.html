Privacy Policy for ProcessDashboard Last Updated: May 2, 2026 1. Introduction ProcessDashboard is designed to help users manage background processes to preserve battery life. We believe in absolute transparency and user-driven control. 2. Permissions & Data Usage Package Usage Stats: This app requires the PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS permission to identify which applications are running in the background. Purpose: This data is used solely to display active processes to the user and calculate potential battery savings. Local Processing: All process analysis is performed locally on your device. 3. Data Collection and Transfer No Collection: ProcessDashboard does not collect, log, or store any personal information. No Third-Party Sharing: We do not transmit any data to external servers, advertisers, or third-party analytics providers. Zero Hacking: The app operates within the standard Android security framework and does not bypass system security. 4. User Control Everything in this app is driven by the user. You have the sole authority to decide which background processes to stop. 5. Contact For questions regarding this policy, please contact the developer via the Google Play Store listing.
